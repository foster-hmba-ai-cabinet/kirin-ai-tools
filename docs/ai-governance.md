# AI Governance: Where to Start

**Purpose:** for students told to help write or evaluate AI policy at work. This page covers frameworks and standards, not tools.

## The short version

- **ISO/IEC 42001:2023** - requirements for an AI management system (AIMS). How an organization governs AI responsibly: roles, risk assessment, controls, continual improvement. Paid standard.
- **ISO/IEC 27090** - guidance on security threats to AI systems: data poisoning, model exfiltration, evasion, membership inference, model inversion, prompt injection, sensitive model output. Guidance rather than requirements, and it covers protecting AI systems rather than using AI for defense. Under publication as of October 2026; check for the published edition. Paid standard.
- **ISO/IEC 27001 / 27002** - the general information security management system and its controls. Both of the above assume this foundation.
- **[NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)** - free, US, voluntary. Govern / Map / Measure / Manage. The lowest-cost starting point and a reasonable first read before buying anything.

## Choosing between them

- **Need certifiable requirements, or a customer is asking what standard you follow:** ISO/IEC 42001.
- **Need to know what you are defending against:** ISO/IEC 27090.
- **Need something free to start a conversation internally:** NIST AI RMF.
- **Already have ISO 27001:** 42001 and 27090 extend it rather than replace it.

## A note on cost

ISO standards are purchased documents, typically a few hundred dollars each. NIST AI RMF is free. Students should check whether UW Libraries provides access to ISO standards before buying anything.
